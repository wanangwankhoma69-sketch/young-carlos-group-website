About Young Carlos Group

Young Carlos Group (YCG) is a proposed founder-led private enterprise, envisioned as one parent organisation coordinating five specialist divisions. Its long-term ambition is to build responsible, innovative ventures with an international outlook.

Our Vision

To become an internationally recognised multidisciplinary group contributing to creativity, knowledge, engineering and enterprise.

Our Mission

To develop original ideas and turn promising concepts into creative works, products, services and sustainable ventures.

Our Founder

Wanangwa Mark Nkhoma

Known creatively as Young Carlos.

Our Five Divisions

Five areas of activity, coordinated under one parent organisation.

01 · Entertainment & Media

Animation, television concepts, film, original characters and creative intellectual property.

02 · Literature & Publishing

Novels, screenplays, educational publications, songwriting and publishing projects.

03 · Technology & Engineering

Technology concepts, product design, electronics, automotive ideas, robotics and software.

04 · Science & Research

Exploration and learning across biology, chemistry, physics and environmental science.

05 · Business & Strategic Development

Entrepreneurship, planning, finance, marketing, partnerships and responsible growth.
